Policy.

This Privacy Policy explains how SaveVido handles information when you use this website.

1. Information We Collect

SaveVido may collect basic technical and usage information, such as website visits, download statistics, and feedback submitted through the website.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to operate, maintain, improve, and understand the performance of SaveVido.

3. Cookies

SaveVido may use cookies or similar technologies for website functionality, analytics, and advertising.

4. Advertising

SaveVido may display advertisements through third-party advertising services such as Google AdSense. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements.

5. Third-Party Services

SaveVido may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, advertising, and media processing. These services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

6. Public Media

SaveVido is designed to work with publicly available media. Users are responsible for making sure that their use or downloading of content complies with applicable laws and the rights of the content owner.

7. Feedback Information

If you submit feedback, your name, message and at least one contact method (email or WhatsApp) are stored in the private SaveVido Neon database for support. A report may also contain the source URL, app/site name and error details when you use the support-note shortcut. This is not account registration; no password or OTP is requested.

Sharing location is optional. If you check the location option and grant the browser permission when submitting feedback, SaveVido stores a one-time location, accuracy and timestamp with that report for troubleshooting. There is no continuous, background or 24/7 tracking. You can deny permission and still send feedback or use SaveVido.

8. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the website. However, no internet service can guarantee complete security.

9. Children's Privacy

SaveVido is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when SaveVido's features, services, or practices change.

11. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please use the feedback channel available on the SaveVido website.
