SAVEVIDO ACCOUNT

Account & privacy

Create an account to save your nickname and privacy choices. Email is used for sign-in and verification. WhatsApp is optional. If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before registering or sharing contact details. WhatsApp and location are not required to use SaveVido.

Create account / Sign in

Email verification is required. Use a strong, unique password. Do not use someone else's email.

Your profile

Your email belongs to your sign-in account. Your WhatsApp number is optional and only saved if you enter it.

Location sharing is off unless you press the button. Your browser asks permission at that moment. Your coordinates are not sent to SaveVido's database; you choose whether to share the generated map link using your device's share sheet.
