YOUR DATA

Privacy Policy.

This notice explains what the SaveVido website and desktop application do with information.

Effective date: 16 November 2026 · Last updated: 16 November 2026

1. Information collected by the website

The public website does not connect directly to the analytics database and does not receive database credentials. Only after you select Allow analytics in the website consent notice does the browser send a limited page-view event to SaveVido's separate analytics service. You can select Decline or clear the saved choice to be asked again. A random browser session identifier is transformed into a SHA-256 hash before storage. The service may derive approximate country and city from hosting request metadata; it does not store raw IP addresses or use GPS. You can decline in the consent notice; you can also clear this site's local storage to reset your choice.

2. Analytics and hosting providers

Website analytics are processed using Neon PostgreSQL and the website is hosted by Vercel. These service providers may process technical connection data, such as IP addresses and request metadata, as part of providing and securing their services under their own policies. SaveVido's analytics tables are designed not to store raw IP addresses.

3. Information handled by the desktop app

The Windows application processes the media URL you enter so it can request information from the source website. Download history, job state, settings and file paths may be stored locally on your device. Downloaded files are saved to the folder shown in the app. Do not enter passwords or share private account cookies with the app.

4. Third-party websites

When you provide a link, the app may communicate with the linked service or its content-delivery providers. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. SaveVido does not control their data practices.

5. Why information is used

  • To operate and secure the website and application.
  • To understand aggregate website usage and service reliability.
  • To save local download history, preferences and resumable job information.
  • To diagnose errors and improve supported functionality.

6. Retention and security

Analytics data is intended to be retained for up to 90 days. Database access is restricted to the private owner dashboard and its server-side API; database credentials are not sent to the public website. Approximate location is based on network metadata and may be inaccurate. No precise GPS location is collected. Reasonable technical safeguards are used, but no internet service or local device can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices

You can clear this website's local storage in your browser to remove its local session identifier. Doing so may create a new anonymous session next time. You can remove local app history or files using the app and your device's file manager; deleting a history entry may not delete the downloaded file itself.

8. Children and sensitive information

Do not use SaveVido to access age-restricted material or submit sensitive personal information. Parents or guardians should supervise minors' use of internet services.

9. Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as features change. The current version will be posted here. A dedicated public privacy contact address has not yet been published; use the in-app feedback option if available.
