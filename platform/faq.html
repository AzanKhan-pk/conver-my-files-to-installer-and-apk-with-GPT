Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about SaveVido, downloads, supported links and troubleshooting.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

What is SaveVido?

SaveVido is a media-downloader project designed to help users save supported public media links in available formats. Features and platform compatibility can change as source websites change.

Which websites are supported?

Support depends on the extraction engine and the source website. Public links may work on services such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Reddit, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch, SoundCloud, Bilibili and Pinterest. This is not a guarantee that every post or format will work.

Why does a link fail?

A post may be private, removed, region-restricted, login-only, DRM-protected or temporarily unsupported. Try an accessible public link and check that the source website permits the intended use.

Why is a quality option unavailable?

SaveVido can only offer formats exposed by the source. Higher resolutions may require separate audio/video streams and FFmpeg. The app should show an error rather than pretend an unavailable format was downloaded.

Where are downloaded files saved?

The Windows app's default folder is Downloads/SaveVido. The app's settings may allow you to choose another folder. Check the location shown in the app before downloading.

Can I pause or resume?

The Windows desktop version includes job controls where supported by the source and downloader. A restart can interrupt an active transfer; saved jobs may be resumed after reopening the app, but not every source supports reliable resumption.

Are Windows and Android installers available now?

Only files shown as published on the official download page should be treated as releases. If a card says Coming soon, there is no verified public installer for that platform yet.

Is SaveVido affiliated with YouTube or other platforms?

No. SaveVido is an independent project and is not endorsed by the services whose links may be supported.

Can I download copyrighted content?

You are responsible for having permission and following copyright law and the source platform's terms. Use SaveVido only for content you own, are authorized to save, or that is otherwise lawful to download.

How do I report a problem?

Use the feedback or support option inside the app if available. A public support email has not yet been published on this website.
